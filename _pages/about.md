Professor Julián Andrés Díaz Táutiva is a scholar specializing in business economics, corporate governance, entrepreneurship, and sustainability, with a particular interest in emerging economies. He holds a Ph.D. in Business Economics, awarded cum laude by Universidad del Desarrollo in Chile, as well as master’s degrees in innovation, entrepreneurship, economics, and marketing from universities in Chile and Spain. He is currently Program Director of the Bachelor of Science in International Business and Research Assistant Professor at Universidad Andrés Bello in Concepción, Chile. His research examines how governance arrangements, entrepreneurial activity, institutional conditions, and climate-related challenges influence organizational strategy and performance.

His work has been published in peer-reviewed journals including Annals of Tourism Research, International Entrepreneurship and Management Journal, Management Review Quarterly, European Research on Management and Business Economics, and Technology in Society. He has also contributed to international research initiatives, including the GLOBE Project, and has received scholarships and research funding from organizations such as Chile’s National Agency for Research and Development (ANID) and the Organization of American States. Beyond his academic work, Professor Díaz connects research with practice through consulting, collaboration with industry, public engagement, and contributions to the media.
